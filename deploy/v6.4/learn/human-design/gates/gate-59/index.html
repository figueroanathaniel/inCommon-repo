Gate 59: Sexuality

The barrier that decides intimacy.

When it overshoots

Intimacy used to secure something.

At its best

Openness chosen deliberately.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Held, the pull toward intimacy is strong; choosing whom to open to is the point.

In a chart where it is open

Where it is open, another person’s wish for closeness may feel intense; the pace is still yours to set.

When it is in the sky

Intimacy, and the barriers to it, are in focus. Openness chosen deliberately deepens connection.

Where would deeper intimacy be welcome, and where would it not?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorIntimacy needs a choice beneath it, so decide whom to open to before opening.
  2. Line 2, The HermitThe pull toward closeness is warm and plain here, and others feel invited by it.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrOpening too quickly to the wrong person teaches, more than any advice could, how to choose.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistIntimacy deepens most safely inside a friendship where trust was built long before anything was asked of it.
  5. Line 5, The HereticCloseness asked for on demand is not closeness; setting your own pace keeps it genuine.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelThe intimacies that mattered were the ones entered freely and kept freely.
