Gate 3: Ordering

New life pressing to be organised.

When it overshoots

Chaos justified as freedom.

At its best

Order that lets change survive.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

For a chart that holds this gate, the confusion of a beginning tends to feel productive, because the knack for sorting new pieces is already in place.

In a chart where it is open

Left open, the pressure to organise something new can feel like another person’s chaos; helping to sort it does not mean taking it home.

When it is in the sky

Something new is trying to get organised in the collective air. Beginnings feel messy, and the mess is how anything new finds its order.

What new thing is still at the untidy stage, and what is the smallest order you could give it?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorOrder begins with one small reliable thing, a habit or a shelf, that holds while everything else is still moving.
  2. Line 2, The HermitSorting the new comes so easily that it hardly registers as a skill, until somebody stuck in the muddle asks how it was done.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrMost of the order here is found by trial, putting pieces in the wrong place until the right arrangement suggests itself.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistA beginning finds its shape faster in company, and one dependable person steadies it better than any amount of solitude.
  5. Line 5, The HereticPeople may look for someone to tidy their confusion; a usable first step is a generous offer, and a full solution is not owed.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelOver time the early mess turns out to have been soil, and order that grew slowly outlasts order that was imposed.

Channels through this gate

  • Mutation · 3-60Energy for change. Pressure for the new, held back until the ordering is possible. Change comes in pulses. With Gate 60, Acceptance.
