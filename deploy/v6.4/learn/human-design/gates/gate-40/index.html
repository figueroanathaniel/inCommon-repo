Gate 40: Aloneness

Work, then withdrawal to recover.

When it overshoots

Denial of needing anyone.

At its best

Rest taken before the collapse.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Held, the need to retreat after giving is strong, and resting before the collapse is the wise order.

In a chart where it is open

With it open, someone else’s need for solitude can feel like rejection; usually it is not.

When it is in the sky

Work followed by rest is favoured. Withdrawal after effort restores rather than retreats.

Where do you need to rest, and have you claimed it?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorRest begins with knowing your limits, so notice the moment effort tips into depletion.
  2. Line 2, The HermitWithdrawal carries no guilt, and a closed door is understood as recovery rather than a slight.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrWorking straight through exhaustion is how this line learns to rest, sometimes by way of a collapse.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistWithdrawal is easier when friends understand that it is recovery rather than rejection.
  5. Line 5, The HereticAvailability without limit is nobody’s to promise; claiming rest out loud keeps relationships honest.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelUnderneath everything else, across a life, sat the plain rhythm of working and stopping.
