Gate 29: Saying Yes

Commitment with the whole body.

When it overshoots

Yes said to avoid the discomfort of no.

At its best

Commitment that follows through.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

In a chart that carries the gate, the capacity for wholehearted commitment is strong; it is worth checking that each yes is meant.

In a chart where it is open

Without the gate, someone else’s enthusiasm may draw a yes out of you before you have decided; a pause protects it.

When it is in the sky

Commitments come easily. Saying yes is powerful, and saying yes to the right thing matters more.

What have you said yes to that you would choose again?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorA yes rests on knowing what it commits to, so read the terms before agreeing to anything.
  2. Line 2, The HermitCommitment comes with the whole body here, and a genuine yes carries itself through without strain.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrA few yeses will be regretted, and each regret teaches what a true one feels like from the inside.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistCommitments are easiest to keep among friends who hold each other to them kindly.
  5. Line 5, The HereticA clear refusal is what keeps the important agreements possible, however much a request expects agreement.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelCounted at the end, the commitments that shaped a life were few, and each was worth everything put into it.

Channels through this gate

  • Discovery · 29-46Succeeding where others fail. Commitment plus a body in the right place. Saying yes and meaning all of it. With Gate 46, Determination.
