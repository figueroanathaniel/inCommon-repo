Gate 51: Shock

The shock that changes the order of things.

When it overshoots

Competition for its own sake.

At its best

Courage that opens something.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Held in the chart, the capacity to meet a jolt with nerve is strong, and competition may be tempting.

In a chart where it is open

Without it natally, a jolt in someone else’s life shakes the floor under yours; finding your footing comes first.

When it is in the sky

Shock and surprise shake the order of things. Courage can open something that politeness would not.

What shock might be opening a door?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorA shock is easier met from firm ground, so know where you stand before the ground moves.
  2. Line 2, The HermitNerve in the face of surprise shows up unasked, and it steadies everyone near it.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrOverreacting to a jolt teaches proportion, usually after the fact.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistA shock settles faster in company, where somebody else can hold the cup steady while the hands still shake.
  5. Line 5, The HereticNobody is unshakeable; an honest reaction to a jolt helps others more than a performance of calm.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelThe shocks that changed things opened doors politeness would have kept shut for good.

Channels through this gate
