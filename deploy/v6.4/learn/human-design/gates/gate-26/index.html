Gate 26: The Egoist

Memory turned into persuasion.

When it overshoots

The useful exaggeration.

At its best

Telling the truth with the same force.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Carried in the chart, the talent for presenting things well is amplified, and telling the truth with the same force is the test.

In a chart where it is open

An open 26 is easily sold to; checking the facts first is simple prudence.

When it is in the sky

Persuasion is strong. A good story sells, and whether it is true matters more than ever.

What would you sell if you could only tell the truth about it?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorPersuasion rests on knowing the facts well enough that no exaggeration is needed.
  2. Line 2, The HermitA memorable way of telling things comes without trying, and listeners lean in long before anything is asked of them.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrOverselling teaches honesty quickly, and the embarrassment is part of the lesson.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistA good account spreads along trusted connections, and their trust is the real currency.
  5. Line 5, The HereticOthers may brace for a pitch; telling the plain truth with conviction surprises them more than any pitch could.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelWith time, the stories that held up were the true ones told with force.
