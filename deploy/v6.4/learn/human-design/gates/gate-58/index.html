Gate 58: Vitality

Joy that presses towards improvement.

When it overshoots

Dissatisfaction as a personality.

At its best

Aliveness that improves things.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Carried in the chart, vitality is strong; channelled into improvement, it stops being restlessness.

In a chart where it is open

Lacking the gate, a lively friend can lift the whole day; let it.

When it is in the sky

Joy presses toward improvement. Aliveness wants to make things better.

What would you improve for the sheer joy of it?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorVitality needs a body to live in, so sleep, food and movement come before any plan for making things better.
  2. Line 2, The HermitAliveness rises easily here, and a whole table brightens around it.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrRestlessness can pass itself off as joy for a while, and telling the two apart is the lesson of the stumble.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistJoy doubles when there is someone to turn to and say look at this, and that turning is half the pleasure.
  5. Line 5, The HereticEnthusiasm expected around the clock turns brittle; honest tiredness is allowed.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelThe joy that lasted was the kind that left something better for others too.

Channels through this gate

  • Judgment · 18-58Insatiability. Correction driven by the wish for improvement. Criticism as a form of love. With Gate 18, Correction.
