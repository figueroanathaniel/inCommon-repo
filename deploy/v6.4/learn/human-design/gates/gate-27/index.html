Gate 27: Caring

Responsibility for other people’s wellbeing.

When it overshoots

Care that will not let go.

At its best

Nourishment offered, not imposed.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Held, the instinct to look after people is amplified, and looking after yourself counts toward it.

In a chart where it is open

Where the gate is open, other people’s need for care can pull hard; giving what can be spared is enough.

When it is in the sky

Care is in the air: nourishing others feels natural, and so does giving too much.

Who are you caring for, and who is caring for you?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorCare starts at home, with your own body and basic needs looked after first.
  2. Line 2, The HermitNourishment is offered here before anyone thinks to ask, and people leave the table better fed than they arrived.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrOver giving teaches the shape of healthy care, usually one depleted stretch at a time.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistCare circulates through a community, and receiving it is part of giving it.
  5. Line 5, The HereticIf endless care is expected, the honest offer is the amount that can be kept up, which protects the giver and the given.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelThe care that counted most in the end taught others to look after themselves.

Channels through this gate

  • Preservation · 27-50Custodianship. Care with rules. Responsibility for others, held as values. With Gate 50, Values.
