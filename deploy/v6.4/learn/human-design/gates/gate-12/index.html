Gate 12: Caution

Expression that only works in the right mood.

When it overshoots

Silence weaponised.

At its best

Words that arrive at the right moment.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Natally present, this gate makes the voice especially sensitive to mood, and waiting for the right moment pays.

In a chart where it is open

Where the gate is open, the urge to speak can arrive on someone else’s clock; a pause before replying is a courtesy to both.

When it is in the sky

Words carry weight, and timing matters more than content. Speech offered in the right mood lands far better.

What would change if you waited for the right mood to say it?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorBefore speaking, read the mood carefully; caution on this line is groundwork rather than fear.
  2. Line 2, The HermitThe right words come when the mood is right and not before, and waiting for them takes no effort at all.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrSpeaking at the wrong moment teaches the right moment faster than any amount of hesitation could.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistWords land best among people who know your moods well enough to hear what sits underneath them.
  5. Line 5, The HereticPeople may want a statement on demand; offering one only when it can be meant keeps the voice trustworthy.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelFrom a distance, the things said at the right time are the ones that lasted, and the silences turn out to have been part of speech.
