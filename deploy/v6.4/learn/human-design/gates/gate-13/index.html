Gate 13: The Listener

Holding other people’s stories without leaking them.

When it overshoots

Collecting secrets for leverage.

At its best

Listening that lets a person be heard.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

A chart that already keeps other people’s stories is offered more of them now, and keeping them well is the gift.

In a chart where it is open

With this gate open, confidences may arrive unexpectedly. Holding a secret carefully is a kindness that costs nothing.

When it is in the sky

Stories surface easily, and people want to be heard. Listening becomes a quiet form of leadership.

Whose story have you not yet fully heard?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorListening well begins with hearing a person out without planning the reply, which is quieter work than it looks.
  2. Line 2, The HermitPeople tell their stories here without being asked, drawn by a kind of attention that does not try to fix anything.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrA confidence or two may be handled clumsily before the art of holding them is learned, and that lesson tends to stick.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistStories pass along trusted friendships, and being a link in that chain is a privilege worth honouring.
  5. Line 5, The HereticA listener can be mistaken for a vault; keeping only what can be kept well is fair to the teller and to you.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelOver years the stories heard become a kind of wisdom, a long view of how people actually live.

Channels through this gate

  • The Prodigal · 13-33A witness. Listening, then retelling. Experience preserved as narrative. With Gate 33, Privacy.
