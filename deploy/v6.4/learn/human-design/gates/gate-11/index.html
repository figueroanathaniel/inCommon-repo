Gate 11: Ideas

Ideas that arrive to be told, not to be acted on.

When it overshoots

Mistaking ideas for instructions.

At its best

Ideas offered lightly.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Carried in the chart, the mind fills with images and possibilities, and talking them through serves better than acting on them.

In a chart where it is open

With no natal hold on this gate, other people’s ideas can feel urgent; enjoy them as stories rather than orders.

When it is in the sky

Ideas are plentiful, more than anyone could act on. They are worth sharing and worth holding lightly.

Which idea is worth telling someone today, and which is only worth enjoying?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorAn idea becomes useful once it is grounded in what is already known, so research the image before sharing it.
  2. Line 2, The HermitIdeas turn up uninvited and in quantity, and they do best let out casually over a meal rather than announced from a stage.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrMany ideas fall apart on contact with the world, and each collapse clears room for one sturdy enough to hold.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistThe right listener turns an idea into something real, so share it first with the friend who likes to build things.
  5. Line 5, The HereticOthers may treat an idea offered in passing as a plan you can be held to; calling it a possibility keeps it light.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelIn the long run the ideas that mattered were the ones other people carried forward, which is the natural fate of a good one.
