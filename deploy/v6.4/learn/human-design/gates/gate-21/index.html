Gate 21: Control

Authority over resources and territory.

When it overshoots

Control for the feel of it.

At its best

Stewardship that gives others room.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

Held, the urge to manage things runs strong, and leaving others some room makes the control lighter to carry.

In a chart where it is open

With the gate open, others may try to manage your territory; deciding what to hand over, and what not, settles it.

When it is in the sky

Control over resources and territory is in question. Stewardship works better than grip.

What do you need to control, and what could you trust to someone else?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorControl starts with knowing exactly what is yours to manage and what never was.
  2. Line 2, The HermitStewardship sits lightly on this line, and a household relaxes under a hand that holds the keys without clenching them.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrThe limits of control are learned by overreaching, and the pushback shows the right size of the job.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistAuthority works best among trusted people, where a task can be handed over without fear.
  5. Line 5, The HereticIf others want their affairs run for them, an arrangement agreed at the start spares everyone the resentment at the end.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelThe finest control in the long run was the kind that slowly made itself unnecessary.

Channels through this gate

  • Money · 21-45A materialist. Control of resources and their distribution. Stewardship with a will behind it. With Gate 45, The Gatherer.
