Human Design · G center

Gate 15: Extremes

A wide human range rather than a fixed pace.

When it overshoots

Extremes performed as personality.

At its best

Range that makes room for others.

Carried and open

In a chart that carries it

For a chart carrying the gate, a wide span of moods and speeds feels natural, and accepting it is easier than fighting it.

In a chart where it is open

Open here, other people’s extremes pull at the schedule; choosing which swings to join keeps the calendar honest.

When it is in the sky

Rhythms swing widely, early and late, all or nothing. The range itself is healthy, and forcing an even pace wastes it.

Where could more range, rather than more consistency, serve you?

The six lines

  1. Line 1, The InvestigatorA wide range rests on a steady centre, so find the few habits that hold through every swing.
  2. Line 2, The HermitA broad span of moods and speeds sits easily here, and an uneven companion feels unexpectedly welcome beside it.
  3. Line 3, The MartyrExtremes teach balance by overshooting it, and every swing too far shows where the middle really is.
  4. Line 4, The OpportunistA wide temperament finds its place among friends who welcome both the early riser and the night owl.
  5. Line 5, The HereticSomeone hoping for a steady metronome will be disappointed; the honest range serves them better than a performance of evenness.
  6. Line 6, The Role ModelOver time the extremes made room for many kinds of people, and that breadth turns out to have been a gift to them.

Channels through this gate

  • Rhythm · 5-15Being in the flow. A fixed pattern meeting an extreme range. Rhythm broken is felt in the body. With Gate 5, Fixed Rhythms.
