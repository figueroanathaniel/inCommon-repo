Animal symbolism

The animals people actually report noticing, each read in three parts kept apart.

  • AlligatorRead as ancient patience and as knowing exactly when to move.
  • AntRead as patience, cooperation and the strength of small repeated effort.
  • BatRead as rebirth and as navigating the dark by other means than sight.
  • BearRead as strength held in reserve, and as the value of a season spent withdrawn.
  • BeaverRead as building, and as the patience to change a landscape one piece at a time.
  • BeeRead as work that only makes sense as a group, and as sweetness earned rather than given.
  • BeetleRead as renewal, especially through the Egyptian scarab, which rolled the sun across the sky.
  • Blue jayRead as a call to speak up, and as a warning about who is being loud on your behalf.
  • ButterflyRead as transformation, and in many traditions as a soul passing or visiting.

  • CoyoteRead as the trickster: the lesson that arrives disguised as an inconvenience.
  • CraneRead as longevity and fidelity, especially in East Asian tradition, where the crane carries a thousand years.
  • CricketRead as luck and as the sound of a house being lived in. Strongly lucky in Chinese tradition.

  • DeerRead as gentleness with alertness: the ability to be soft and watchful at the same time.
  • DogRead as loyalty, protection, and a reminder of who is actually on your side.
  • DolphinRead as play, breath, and help arriving from an unexpected direction.
  • DoveRead as peace, and as a message arriving gently rather than loudly.
  • DragonflyRead as clarity arriving after a long unclear period, and as living lightly on the surface of things.
  • DuckRead as staying steady on the surface while a good deal happens underneath.
  • EagleRead as sovereignty and clear sight, and in many traditions as a carrier between the human and the divine.
  • FalconRead as focus and decisive speed: knowing the moment and taking it without a second pass.

  • FrogRead as cleansing and change, and as the sound that means the rain has come.
  • GooseRead as loyalty and as the discipline of a group: taking a turn at the front and then falling back.
  • GrasshopperRead as a leap taken forward, and as movement that cannot be made in small steps.
  • GullRead as adaptability and as a reminder to stop carrying what you can set down.
  • HawkRead as perspective: the long view, the thing you can see from height that you cannot see from the road.
  • HeronRead as patience and self possession: standing still on purpose while everything moves.
  • HorseRead as freedom and drive, and in older traditions as the carrier between one world and the next.
  • HummingbirdRead as joy, and as a reminder that the small and quick is not the same as the unimportant.
  • LadybugRead as luck arriving, and as a small good thing that should not be brushed off.
  • LizardRead as regeneration and as letting go of what has been caught.

  • OpossumRead as strategy over confrontation: playing dead, waiting, and leaving when it is safe.

  • Wild turkeyRead as abundance and as the return of something that was nearly lost.
  • WolfRead as loyalty inside a group, and as instinct that has not been argued out of itself.
  • WoodpeckerRead as persistence, and as a signal to keep knocking on the thing you have been knocking on.
  • WormRead as unseen work that everything above ground depends on.
