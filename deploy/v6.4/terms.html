About inCommon

inCommon is a space for self-reflection. It brings astrology, Human Design, numerology and other reflective traditions together with a private journal, so you can look at your own patterns over time and notice what you hold in common with the people around you.

You can begin with nothing more than a birth date. Your charts, your readings and your record live with you: most of inCommon works without an account, and an account lets you keep your record across devices. The Oracle writes personal readings for the day, the week and the month, and a Cycle Audit of the long passages the slowest astral bodies make across your chart. Codex Luminary is an optional single payment, of an amount you choose, that opens the add-ons: in-depth readings, Omni tarot and the Cycle Audit's readings. A library of reference material, reflective practices, readings and a journal sit alongside them.

inCommon is made for reflection and personal exploration. It is not a source of predictions, and it is not a substitute for professional care or advice of any kind. If you are ever in crisis, Get Help is on every screen.

inCommon is created and maintained by Nathaniel J. Figueroa.
