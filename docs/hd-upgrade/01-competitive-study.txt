1. WHAT THIS IS, AND HOW FAR TO TRUST IT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The owner asked for a study of Ra.TV and maiamechanics.com: what they offer,
how it compares with inCommon, the quality of each, and what it would take to
double their quality and overall experience, with pros and cons drawn from
customer reviews.

Sources used:
  - The official pages: ra.tv, maiamechanics.com, jovianarchive.com (the
    desktop Maia Mechanics Imaging product page, the FAQ).
  - One independent review: Vaness Henry's calculator round up. The author
    is an affiliate of a competitor (Genetic Matrix) and wrote a rival
    Variable calculator, so read it as informed but not neutral.
  - An assessment the owner pasted in, written by another assistant from
    Reddit threads and the official sites. Its code claims were re-checked
    against the repository here (section 5).
  - inCommon's own source and gates, run on main 53d792a.

Limits, stated plainly:
  - CUSTOMER REVIEW EVIDENCE IS THIN. No Trustpilot page exists for Jovian
    Archive, Ra.TV or Maia Mechanics. Ra.TV's app store listings carry too
    few ratings to score. Reddit, where most real user talk happens, is closed
    to the tools this study could use, so the Reddit findings in the pasted
    assessment are reported here as that assessment's, not re-verified.
  - Neither paid product was used hands on. Everything about them is what
    they publish.
  - So the pros and cons below are weighted: published capability first,
    the one independent review second, reported user comments third. Where a
    claim rests on one person, it says so.

3. WHAT THEIR CUSTOMERS SAY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ra.TV
  For       Depth, and direct access to the founder's own teaching, at a
            price well under the value of the archive. (Pasted assessment,
            Reddit; consistent with the product's design.)
  Against   One launch era user missed translations. A single, old report;
            not a current pattern. No other criticism could be found, which
            says as much about how little review data exists as about the
            product.

Maia Mechanics
  For       Professional depth: gate, line, color, tone and base laid out
            visually; the birth time reliability tool; dark mode; offline
            library; the field's own terminology, which practitioners value
            (independent review; pasted assessment).
  Against   Not for beginners (independent review). Bulk migration from the
            desktop library was hard for one paying user, who spent real
            time on it; their follow up is the same incident, not a second
            one. Printing and PDF export sit behind payment, which workshop
            users found friction for quick sharing. Support reports were
            mixed, and others disagreed with the complaint. One missing
            symbol bug was reported fixed. (All from the pasted assessment.)

What the reviews teach, as design rules:
  1. Faithful terminology matters to practitioners. Plain language must
     sharpen the field's meaning, never replace it.
  2. Data has to move: import, export, migrate, without pain.
  3. A readable, conventional chart has to exist beside any immersive one.
     Some readers find unfamiliar colours and layouts hard to read.
  4. Sharing a chart (a PDF, a handout) should be easy and not paywalled.
  5. Beginners and professionals are both underserved: Ra.TV teaches but
     does not compute; Maia computes but does not teach.

5. THE PASTED ASSESSMENT, CHECKED AGAINST THE CODE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Confirmed:
  - Atlas counts: 15 sections, 64 gates, 36 channels, 9 centres, 12
    profiles, 6 line archetypes (hd-atlas.js, read directly).
  - Color, Tone and Base are not computed. hd-atlas.js says so itself:
    "This build reads to gate and line only."
  - The design moment falls back to a flat 88.36 days when the solver is
    missing (app, designT; arc-solver.js header). It is a fallback for a
    missing module, which CLAUDE.md records as deliberate, but nothing on
    the screen says a chart was drawn from it.
  - A profile helper hides bad data. HDAtlas.profileFor() normalises an
    impossible pair into a valid profile: 1/5 reads as 1/4, 6/1 as 6/2, 3/3
    as 3/5. And hdProfile() in the app falls back to line 1 and line 3 when
    either Sun is missing, so a missing activation becomes a 1/3.
  - The sleep teaching is wrong, and in FOUR places, not one. hd-life.js
    line 163 (the defined Sacral), 238 (Generator), 244 (Manifesting
    Generator) and 303 (Sacral authority) all say, in effect, "go to bed
    before you are tired". In the tradition that is the advice for those
    without a defined Sacral (line 173, the open Sacral, gives it correctly);
    a defined Sacral goes to bed once the energy is spent. This is a
    teaching error, not a medical claim either way.
  - 2 gate and 23 line changes across 19,750 reference positions
    (check-astral-bodies.js). The check itself calls these reference
    boundary crossings, not failures: positions inside 0.03 degrees of a
    boundary, where the library and Horizons land on opposite sides.

Corrected:
  - "All 384 gate specific line interpretations remain to be written" is
    wrong. timing-lexicon.js already holds all 384 (GATE_LINE, held to
    originality by TL6b and TL15). They are written for transits on Today,
    in the time neutral voice that layer needs; what is missing is a natal
    reading of each line inside the Atlas and the chart, which may reuse,
    adapt or stand beside them, and that is an editorial decision.

Added by this study:
  - Why the 0.03 degree ceiling matters for the next layer down. A line is
    0.9375 deg; a color is a sixth of that, 0.156 deg; a tone 0.026 deg; a
    base 0.0052 deg. Color is safely computable from today's positions.
    Tone sits at the ephemeris's own error for the Moon. Base is below it,
    and the Moon crosses a base in about 37 seconds of birth time. So Color
    can be shown as a fact, Tone only with a stated confidence, and Base
    only where the birth time is known to the second, which is almost never.
    This is where inCommon can beat Maia honestly: show the reliability of
    every sub line value rather than printing a base nobody can know.
  - Trademarks. CLAUDE.md's competitor surface gate bars marks such as
    bodygraph as a surface name, and Penta and BG5 are Jovian product names.
    Any group or business feature needs inCommon's own names (Circle already
    exists) and its own writing. Ra Uru Hu's teaching is copyrighted: it can
    be pointed to, never hosted or paraphrased closely (the I Ching lesson in
    CLAUDE.md applies).


6. WHAT DOUBLING THEM TAKES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Twice the quality" needs a measure, or it is a slogan. Proposed, and none
of these has been demonstrated yet:

  Outcome                              Target
  ------------------------------------ ---------------------------------------
  Find the right explanation           half the competitor's time, same or
                                       better correctness
  Remember it a week later             half the conceptual errors at 7 days
  Make a client handout                half the time, complete data
  Explain chart relationships          twice as many correct, fixed session
  Integrity                            every chart inspectable; every sub
                                       line value carries its reliability;
                                       every teaching passage has a source,
                                       a reviewer and a correction history

Measured with about 30 people across beginner, student and practitioner,
counterbalanced, learning tasks against Ra.TV and chart tasks against Maia.

The work, in four pillars:

PILLAR A. A CHART YOU CAN INSPECT AND TRUST
  - Show the conversion: local birth time, zone and offset, UT, the design
    moment and its solved arc, and which convention each step used.
  - Never let a fallback become a reading: the 88.36 day fallback, a
    missing Sun and an impossible profile all surface as provisional or
    unavailable, with the evidence kept.
  - Birth time study that beats Maia's: scan the hours either side, show
    which properties hold, which change and when, and let the reader open
    the alternative chart at any change, all with the reliability of each
    sub line value stated.
  - Color, Tone and Base computed, each with its confidence; Variables from
    them, shown only when the time supports them.
  - Reference cases: a set of public, consented or historical charts
    checked against independent calculators, with every disagreement
    explained (zone revisions, missing places, boundary crossings).

PILLAR B. A REVIEWED TEACHING SYSTEM
  - Fix the four sleep passages first.
  - Natal line readings for all 384 inside the Atlas and the chart, crosses
    (192 Incarnation Crosses as named in the tradition, written fresh),
    color, tone and base, and chart synthesis.
  - Every substantive passage carries a source, a named human reviewer with
    Human Design training, and a correction history. Software cannot judge
    fidelity; a reviewer role is required, and the owner appoints it.
  - Beginner, study and practitioner depth for the same entry.
  - Original writing only; permissions for anything quoted.

PILLAR C. THE CHART IS THE STUDY WORKSPACE
  - Select a gate: its explanation opens beside the chart, the chart stays.
  - Select a passage: the structure it describes lights up on the chart.
  - The worked journey: a hanging gate, why it is active, its partner, the
    channel it would complete, the line reading, a note saved to that gate.
  - Notes, saved chart versions, searchable lessons, an accessible text
    alternative for every graphic, and a conventional 2D chart always one
    tap away beside the 3D.
  - Oki answers from the chart's structured data and approved sources only,
    and says when a question is outside them.

PILLAR D. PROFESSIONAL RELIABILITY
  - Import (including Maia's .mmi and common CSV shapes), export, a free
    readable PDF and an anonymised client handout.
  - Offline that holds, recovery from interruption, client data permissions
    and sharing done by workstream 2's rules.
  - Groups and time study as specialist modules, each with its own
    calculation and editorial review, under inCommon's own names.

Phases and estimate (planning figures, not commitments):
  Phase 1, 4 to 6 weeks: Pillar A's inspection and honesty work, the sleep
    fix, reference cases, the conventional 2D view beside 3D, free PDF.
  Phase 2, 6 to 8 weeks: the study workspace (Pillar C) and natal line
    readings; birth time scanner; Color with confidence.
  Phase 3, 2 to 6 months: Tone, Base and Variables with reliability;
    crosses; import; groups and time study; the 30 person benchmark.
  A strong first release is 12 to 20 weeks with two experienced engineers,
  design and QA support, an editor, and independent Human Design reviewers.
  Broad specialist coverage is 6 to 12 months, and content review and
  permissions are likely to set the pace rather than code.

A claim to be the most comprehensive in the world also needs myBodyGraph,
Genetic Matrix, Neutrino Design, Humdes, 64keys and Human.Design examined.
This study sets priorities; it does not establish superiority.
