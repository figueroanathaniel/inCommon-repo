THE OUTCOME
Every Human Design chart inCommon draws says how it was made and never passes
a guess off as a fact. Then the first layer below the line, Color, is
computed with its confidence, and a reader with an uncertain birth time can
see exactly what that uncertainty changes.

TASK 1. FIX THE SLEEP TEACHING (branch feature/hd-sleep)
  Four passages in app/hd-life.js give a defined Sacral the advice the
  tradition gives to those WITHOUT one ("go to bed before you are tired, let
  the energy burn off lying down"): line 163 (the defined Sacral), 238
  (Generator), 244 (Manifesting Generator, "the same rule as the
  Generator") and 303 (Sacral authority). A defined Sacral goes to bed once
  its energy is used up. Rewrite all four in the file's own voice. The open
  Sacral passage (line 173, "lie down before they do") is right and stays.
  Read every other sleep passage in the file for the same fault, and list
  each one you checked in the PR. No medical claim either way. Mark the PR "needs
  Human Design review" (delegation plan, D1 and D2).

TASK 2. NO SILENT FALLBACKS (branch feature/hd-provisional)
  a. designT(): when the arc solver is missing or fails, the chart is drawn
     from the flat 88.36 day offset. Keep the fallback, and carry a flag on
     the chart (for example hd.provisional = ['design']) that the chart
     screens show as one plain sentence near the chart: what was
     approximated and why. No sentence when nothing was.
  b. hdProfile(): a missing personality or design Sun currently becomes line
     1 or line 3. Return the profile as unavailable instead, and the screens
     say so where the profile would be.
  c. HDAtlas.profileFor() (hd-atlas.js): an impossible pair (1/5, 6/1, 3/3)
     is normalised into a real profile. Keep normalizeProfile for the
     callers that need it, but make profileFor return the raw pair and
     valid:false for an impossible one, and have the app show it as
     unavailable. A real chart can never produce one, so seeing it means
     the data is wrong, and hiding that is the fault.
  Tests: a module row for (c) in tools/run-module-tests.js, and rows in the
  harness (coordinate with workstream 6) for (a) and (b) with the solver
  removed and a Sun missing. Each shown red against the current code.

TASK 3. THE INSPECTION PANEL (branch feature/hd-inspect)
  One panel, opened from the chart in both shells, that shows how this chart
  was made: the birth date and time as given, the zone and its offset on
  that date, UT, the personality instant, the design instant and the solar
  arc it was solved to, the iterations, the ephemeris used, and any
  provisional flags from Task 2. Numbers and labels only; no explanation of
  what a reading means (CLAUDE.md, "Discovery belongs to the reader"). It is
  a dialog, so it follows the focus trap and announcement rules.

TASK 4. COLOR, WITH ITS CONFIDENCE (branch feature/hd-color, after Tasks 2
and 3 merge and after workstream 6 has landed its reference cases)
  A new mechanics module, app/hd-subline.js, classified in
  check-layer-boundary.js as mechanics, computing color (and only color in
  this task) from a longitude, the way hd-wheel.js computes gate and line,
  with the same half open boundary rule. It also returns a confidence: the
  distance to the nearest color boundary against the body's measured
  ephemeris error and the angular change the birth time's stated precision
  allows. A color within that margin is returned as uncertain, with both
  candidates. Gate, line and color appear together in the inspection panel
  and in the gate's detail. Tone and Base are NOT in this task.
  Tests: a module test group for hd-subline.js, including boundary sweeps
  like hd-wheel's twelve rows, and a row that moves the birth time by one
  minute and checks the Moon's confidence drops.

TASK 5. THE BIRTH TIME SCANNER (branch feature/hd-scanner, after Task 4)
  For a chart, scan the hours either side of the given time (default 12,
  as Maia does) and list every moment a major property changes: Type,
  Authority, Profile, definition, any defined channel, and the Ascendant's
  sign for the astrology side. Each change opens the alternative chart. With
  no birth time, the scan covers the whole day and is the honest answer to
  "what do I know without a time". The scan is a pure function of the
  birth record; it may be slow, so agree a budget with workstream 5 (5.2)
  and run it off the render path.
