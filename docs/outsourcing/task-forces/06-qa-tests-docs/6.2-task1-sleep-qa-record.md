## What the gate asserts

`tools/check-hd-sleep.js` builds eight charts, calls the real
`HDTeachings.build(chart, HDAtlas, HDLife)`, and reads the assembled Sleep tab.
The distinction the correction draws is between a **defined Sacral** and an open
one, not between authorities, so the cases include two designs that hold
emotional authority and a defined Sacral at the same time.

| Row | Chart | Type | Authority | Defined centres | Closing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Generator | Generator | Sacral | Sacral, Root, Spleen | post-spending |
| S2 | Generator, emotional | Generator | Emotional | Sacral, Solar Plexus, Root | post-spending |
| S3 | Manifesting Generator | Manifesting Generator | Sacral | Sacral, Root, Throat, G | post-spending |
| S4 | Manifesting Generator, emotional | Manifesting Generator | Emotional | Sacral, Solar Plexus, Throat, G | post-spending |
| S5 | Manifestor | Manifestor | Emotional | Solar Plexus, Throat, Heart | horizontal |
| S6 | Projector, emotional | Projector | Emotional | Solar Plexus, Ajna | horizontal |
| S7 | Projector, splenic | Projector | Splenic | Spleen, Ajna | horizontal |
| S8 | Reflector | Reflector | Lunar | none | horizontal |

Each of S1 to S8 asserts five things about that one reading: the type it names,
the authority it names with that authority's own sleep passage, every sleep
centre named in the state the chart holds it, the closing for that chart's
Sacral state, and the absence of the other design's instruction anywhere in the
tab, bullet lists included.

| Row | What it holds |
|---|---|
| S9 | no reading names a centre in a state the chart does not hold, over all eight |
| S10 | the defined-Sacral sentence appears in the four defined-Sacral readings and in none of the other four |
| S11 | the two `strict` sleep bullet rows appear twice in the four defined-Sacral readings and never in the other four |
| S12 | the open-Sacral passage is untouched and appears in the four readings without a defined Sacral, in the open-centre paragraph, and in none of the four with one |
| S13 | a chart that arrives without a defined list still composes, closes on the horizontal instruction, claims no defined Sacral, and does not throw; a missing chart or atlas returns no tabs |

S11 is the row the review asked QA for: for each non-Sacral shape, neither
defined-Sacral sleep row appears. It is written over all four non-Sacral shapes
rather than three, because the assignment's case list splits Projector into an
emotional and a splenic chart.

No row bans a phrase outright and no row is a snapshot. Every assertion is about
which passage was selected for which chart, which is the fault class this
correction is about. Whether the passages themselves are faithful to the
tradition is a source review, cited in `3.2-task1-sleep-record.md` from Jovian
Archive's Sleeping Mechanics entry, and it is still open below.

## Open defects and limits

1. **Fallback contradiction, unreachable from the app, not fixed here.** A chart
   with neither a `defined` nor an `open` list composes a reading whose third
   paragraph says no centre is defined and whose fourth says every centre is
   defined. The app's `hdChart()` always supplies `open`, so no reader can reach
   it; S13 asserts the reachable part (no throw, horizontal closing, no Sacral
   claim) and this note records the rest rather than pretending it is covered.
2. **`deploy/v6.2` and `deploy/v6.3` still carry the old wording**, and
   `preview/fonts-self/app.html` still carries the four old strings. The
   implementation record calls the two bundles records and the preview snapshot
   out of scope. This gate reads `app/` only, so nothing here claims those
   bundles are correct.
3. **`app/inCommonApp v2.dc.html` and the two `celestial-codex` helpers carry
   their own copy of the authority derivation.** No row here reaches it. The
   authority order it uses (Solar Plexus before Sacral) is the tradition's
   order, and Jovian Archive's Emotional Authority page states it plainly:
   "Anyone with a defined Solar Plexus Center has Emotional Authority,
   regardless of what other Centers are defined. The Solar Plexus overrides
   everything else." An uncommitted change in the work tree reversed that order
   in six files; it was found at the start of this run, preserved as a patch,
   and reverted, because it is a production copy change, it belongs to no QA
   assignment, and it contradicts the tradition. Nothing of it was committed.
4. **The Human Design review marker is still open.** `3.2-task1-hd-review-sheet.md`
   is the sheet for it. Technical readiness and "needs Human Design review" are
   different statements and this page separates them.
