THE OUTCOME
The chart is the study workspace: whatever the reader selects on the chart
opens its explanation without losing the chart, and whatever they read lights
up on the chart.

TASK 1. SELECT, AND KEEP THE CHART (both views)
  Selecting a gate, channel or centre in the 2D wiring or the 3D scene opens
  its Atlas entry beside the chart, not over it, in both shells. On the
  phone that is a sheet that leaves the chart visible above it.

TASK 2. READ, AND SEE IT ON THE CHART
  The reverse link: a gate, channel or centre named in an open Atlas entry,
  a line reading or a note highlights the same structure on the chart, in
  2D and 3D. One highlight model shared by both views.

TASK 3. THE READABLE VIEW IS ALWAYS ONE TAP AWAY
  Users of other platforms name unfamiliar colours and layouts as a reason
  they could not read a chart. The 2D wiring is the conventional, readable
  view; it stays one tap from the 3D everywhere, and the selection carries
  across when switching.

TASK 4. A TEXT ALTERNATIVE FOR EVERY GRAPHIC
  Everything the chart shows is reachable as a structured list for a screen
  reader and for keyboard use, with the same selection and highlight.
