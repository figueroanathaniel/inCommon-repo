THE OUTCOME
A data model, agreed before any screen is built, for what a serious Human
Design student and a practitioner keep: notes on parts of a chart, versions of
a chart, charts of other people, and the files that move charts in and out.

TASK 1. THE MODEL (documents first; commits prefixed "1.2:" on the Task
  Force 1 branch, CHARTER.md)
  data-contracts/HD-STUDY-MODEL.md, covering:
  a. Notes: a note attached to an address in a chart (a gate, a channel, a
     centre, a line, an activation), with the chart's identity, the text,
     dates, and the consent category it belongs to. What happens to notes
     when the birth data changes and the address moves or disappears.
  b. Chart versions: a corrected birth time is a new version, not an
     overwrite; the old one stays readable with its notes.
  c. Charts of other people: the existing saved people (people-library.js,
     the twelve person cap) extended or joined, and how a practitioner's
     client list would differ. Device only or synced is the owner's call
     (as G10 was); present the options.
  d. Import and export: the fields an import must carry (name, date, time
     and its precision, place and its coordinates and zone), how a chart
     with no time or a revised zone is marked, Maia Mechanics' .mmi and a
     plain CSV as the first two formats (document their shapes from public
     information only), and an export format inCommon controls.
  e. Every new key under STORAGE-MIGRATION-POLICY.md, every new table with
     RLS enabled and no policies (workstream 2 writes them, 2.2).

TASK 2. AFTER THE OWNER AGREES THE MODEL
  Migrations and the client side storage, in the order the model names,
  one commit series each, prefixed "1.2:", on the Task Force 1 branch, each tested on the local Postgres with the stubbed auth
  schema as before.

FILES THIS ASSIGNMENT OWNS

  profile-manager.js and people-library.js storage changes.

DONE MEANS

  updated.

SECONDARY AND TERTIARY

  every new piece of data has a consent category, a deletion path and an
  export path.
