## What you are being asked to do

Read the eight claims below against the tradition's own wording, and mark each
one **confirm**, **amend** or **reject**. Then sign the last table.

- **Confirm** means you would stand behind the claim as written, attributed to
  the tradition as it is here.
- **Amend** means the direction is right and the sentence is not. Write the
  wording you would keep, and say which part of the tradition carries it.
- **Reject** means the claim is not the tradition's teaching, or is being put to
  a chart it does not belong to.

Confirming closes the marker **for the sleep scope only**. It says nothing
about the other nine readings, the app, or the chart calculation.

## The tradition's words, as read on 28 October 2026

Human Design Dictionary, **Sleeping Mechanics**
(https://jovianarchive.com/pages/human-design-dictionary):

> The correct approach to sleep is essential to the well being of everyone. Sleep
> approach varies depending on whether you are Sacral-defined or not. Generators,
> the only Sacral beings, according to their mechanics should only go to sleep
> when they are exhausted. Non-Sacral beings, Manifestors, Projectors and
> Reflectors, according to their mechanics should assume a horizontal position
> long before sleep.

Generator page (https://jovianarchive.com/pages/generator-human-design):
"Daily exhaustion is healthy for Sacral beings"; "If you go to bed with Sacral
energy still revving because you spent the day forcing and resisting, sleep is
poor"; "Going to bed without using up your energy. Spend the Sacral through
correct activity. Sleep when truly tired."

**The distinction the whole correction rests on.** Within this tradition, a
defined Sacral sleeps once the day's energy has been spent, and a design without
a defined Sacral takes a horizontal position long before sleep. The early rest
teaching is not a worse version of the Sacral one and not a general rule. It
belongs to the designs it was written for.

## The boundaries the correction was held to

Each of these is a rule the writing was held to, and each is checkable in the
text above:

1. **No bedside instruction.** Nothing here sets a bedtime, a number of hours or
   a time to lie down.
2. **No forced exhaustion and no sleep deprivation.** "Sleep when truly tired"
   is not written as a target to reach by withholding rest.
3. **No health outcome.** The tradition's own framing is kept, and the app's
   Health reading still says in its own words that a tradition of
   self-observation is not a diagnosis.
4. **Attribution rather than assertion.** The sentences say what the tradition
   holds, not what is true of the reader.
5. **The open Sacral entry is deliberately unchanged:** "You need more rest than
   the people around you, and often need to lie down before they do. That is not
   a weakness to be trained out." It is the non-Sacral teaching and it was
   already right. If you think it is wrong, that is a finding, and this task did
   not touch it.
