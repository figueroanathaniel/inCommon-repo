## The teaching, as the tradition states it

Source: Jovian Archive, Human Design Dictionary, **Sleeping Mechanics**.

> The correct approach to sleep is essential to the well being of everyone. Sleep
> approach varies depending on whether you are Sacral-defined or not. Generators,
> the only Sacral beings, according to their mechanics should only go to sleep
> when they are exhausted. Non-Sacral beings, Manifestors, Projectors and
> Reflectors, according to their mechanics should assume a horizontal position
> long before sleep.

Source: Jovian Archive, Generator page. "Daily exhaustion is healthy for Sacral
beings"; "If you go to bed with Sacral energy still revving because you spent the
day forcing and resisting, sleep is poor"; "Going to bed without using up your
energy. Spend the Sacral through correct activity. Sleep when truly tired."

So the defined-Sacral teaching is sleep **after** the day's energy has been spent.
The early horizontal-rest teaching belongs to **non-Sacral** designs. Neither
passage is a bedtime, an instruction to force exhaustion, or a health outcome.

## The minimum composition change

`CLOSE.sleep` is now chosen on **defined Sacral**, not on authority:

    sleep: c.defined.indexOf('Sacral') !== -1 ? <sacral closing> : <non sacral closing>

That is the whole logic change. It is what makes an emotional Generator and an
emotional Manifesting Generator receive the Sacral closing while their emotional
authority passage keeps its own, separate instruction. Sacral definition and
Sacral authority are different facts and are read from different fields.
