## The teaching, and where it was checked

- https://jovianarchive.com/pages/generator-human-design
- https://jovianarchive.com/pages/human-design-dictionary

Read on 28 October 2026. The dictionary's **Sleeping Mechanics** entry says
Generators "should only go to sleep when they are exhausted", and that
non-Sacral beings, meaning Manifestors, Projectors and Reflectors, "should
assume a horizontal position long before sleep". The Generator page adds that
the damage it names is going to bed with the Sacral "still revving", meaning
energy raised during the day and never spent.

So within that tradition the defined-Sacral teaching is sleep after the day's
energy has been spent, and the early horizontal rest is the non-Sacral
teaching. Both passages in the app now attribute the claim to the tradition and
prescribe no bedtime, no forced exhaustion, no sleep deprivation and no health
outcome.

## Sacral definition is not Sacral authority

The closing is selected on `c.defined.indexOf('Sacral')`, never on
`c.authority`. An emotional Generator and an emotional Manifesting Generator
have a defined Sacral and an emotional authority, so they receive the
sacral closing and keep the emotional passages untouched. That is why the
readings below include both, and why the three Sacral closings and the three
non-Sacral closings are visibly different in the evidence file.

## Second pass: the backfill leak the review caught

The review found that `pick()` pads a short list to three items from rows whose
condition did not match, so the Generator-only sleep opportunity ("Ending the
day with the energy spent on something the gut said yes to") reached Projector,
Reflector and Manifestor readings whose closing correctly told them to become
horizontal long before sleep. The challenge row rewritten in the same bank had
the identical fault, and Kimi named the opportunity.

Fix: a bank row may carry a third element, `'strict'`, and `pick()` skips
strict rows when padding. Those two sleep rows carry it. Nothing else changes:
every other bank and every other tab selects exactly as before, because the
review's correction was scoped to this bank and a wider change to selection
would be a feature.

Evidence after the fix, over the same six shapes: every sleep quad still holds
three items; the defined-Sacral rows appear in the three Sacral readings and in
none of the three non-Sacral ones; mismatched readings, counted as a unique
condition appearing on the wrong side of Sacral definition, are 0. The readings
file was regenerated at this commit.

Tradeoff, stated rather than hidden: padding is now a visible choice instead of
a rule. A future chart shape that matches only two sleep rows will read two,
which is honest and is why the marker is documented at `pick()`. The regression
row for this belongs with QA, alongside the three already specified in the
delivery record: for each of the three non-Sacral shapes, assert that neither
defined-Sacral sleep row appears.
