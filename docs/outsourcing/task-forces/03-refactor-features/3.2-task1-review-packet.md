## The teaching, and where it was checked

- https://jovianarchive.com/pages/generator-human-design
- https://jovianarchive.com/pages/human-design-dictionary

Read on 28 October 2026. The dictionary's **Sleeping Mechanics** entry says
Generators "should only go to sleep when they are exhausted", and that
non-Sacral beings, meaning Manifestors, Projectors and Reflectors, "should
assume a horizontal position long before sleep". The Generator page adds that
the damage it names is going to bed with the Sacral "still revving", meaning
energy raised during the day and never spent.

So within that tradition the defined-Sacral teaching is sleep after the day's
energy has been spent, and the early horizontal rest is the non-Sacral
teaching. Both passages in the app now attribute the claim to the tradition and
prescribe no bedtime, no forced exhaustion, no sleep deprivation and no health
outcome.

## Sacral definition is not Sacral authority

The closing is selected on `c.defined.indexOf('Sacral')`, never on
`c.authority`. An emotional Generator and an emotional Manifesting Generator
have a defined Sacral and an emotional authority, so they receive the
sacral closing and keep the emotional passages untouched. That is why the
readings below include both, and why the three Sacral closings and the three
non-Sacral closings are visibly different in the evidence file.
