# Co-Star

Astrology app. Referenced in the studies of 27 and 28 August 2026.

## The finding worth keeping

Their largest single growth driver was **friend-to-friend chart comparison**,
not the daily horoscope. The horoscope is the visible product; the friends layer
is the mechanic. It is a retention loop and a viral loop in one primitive, and
it is computed rather than authored, so it costs nothing per user to run.

That finding is the reason the People Library sits ahead of composite math in
the current ordering. See `../jovian-archive/2026-08-28-phase-1-roadmap.md`,
where the incumbent reached the same conclusion independently.

## What is explicitly not taken

**The visual identity, and the voice.** Both studies name Co-Star in a
do-not-imitate list alongside CHANI and MyBodyGraph: original type, colour and
layout only.

The voice is the more important half, and it is a product decision rather than a
legal one. Co-Star's register is aphoristic, declarative and often cutting, and
it says things about the reader as though they were settled. inCommon's
constraints forbid exactly that: the third layer of every reading is a question
only the reader can close, never a statement about who they are. Two products
can compute the same contact and one of them tells you what you are like while
the other asks you something. That difference is the product.

## Also not taken

Streaks and completeness meters. Category standard, and excluded here by the
broken-streak-pressure constraint.
