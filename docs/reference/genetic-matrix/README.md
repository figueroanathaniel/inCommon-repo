# Genetic Matrix

Human Design chart software, independent of Jovian Archive. Referenced in the
Relational Charting study of 27 August 2026.

## What it was used for

As one of two independent checks on the **connection mechanics**: the four ways
two charts can meet on a single channel. The other check was Jovian's own
published partnership-analysis material.

The point of checking two independent sources was to establish that the four
types are **functional terms of art in general use** rather than one company's
proprietary vocabulary. That is what makes them safe to use as type names:

| Both hold | Type |
|---|---|
| One gate each, different gates | Electromagnetic |
| Both hold the whole channel | Companionship |
| One holds it whole, the other holds neither gate | Dominance |
| One holds it whole, the other holds one gate | Compromise |

## What that permission does not extend to

**The interpretive text.** The types may be named; the readings must be written
here. `hd-composite.js` takes its channel names, themes and meanings from
`hd-atlas.js`, which is inCommon's own writing, and generates its fact lines
from the arithmetic.

## The safety note attached to the same finding

"Dominance" is standard in the field and describes a channel configuration. Read
carelessly next to a real relationship it can validate a coercive dynamic or
manufacture suspicion in a healthy one. Every dominance and compromise row in
the build carries a visible sentence saying the word is about a shape between
two charts and says nothing about how either person treats the other, and no
person is ever labelled dominant. That is asserted by the module gate rather
than left to the copy.
