# CHANI

Astrology app. Referenced in the study of 28 August 2026.

## The finding

Named in the do-not-imitate list, alongside Co-Star and MyBodyGraph: no
imitation of the visual identity. Original type, colour and layout only.

That is the whole of the recorded finding, and it is worth keeping short rather
than padding. What has not been studied here is their content model, their
pricing, or their retention mechanics. If any of those become relevant, study
them then and date the entry.

## The standing rule this belongs to

inCommon's visual identity is its own and is documented in
`docs/web-design-system.md` and in the theme boundary section of `CLAUDE.md`.
Four identities and a contrast modifier, all built from tokens, with a generated
sky rather than a stock starfield precisely because a stock starfield is the
thing every app in this category ships and it reads as one.

Resemblance is a risk in two directions and only one of them is legal. The other
is that a product that looks like the category gets read as the category.
