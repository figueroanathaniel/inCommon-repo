# humandesign.io: the published phasing, and what Phase 1 contains

**Read:** 28 August 2026. **Source:** the founder's study of the same date.

## What they published

| Phase | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1 | Personal chart and analysis, connection charts, real-time transits, AI assistant, and a chart library for friends, family and colleagues. iOS, Android, web. |
| 2 | Educational content |
| 3 | Live sessions with professional analysts |
| 4 | Expert marketplace, booking with certified professionals |

## The signal

The multi-person chart library is in **Phase 1**, ahead of education and ahead
of every surface they intend to charge for. An organisation that has pushed all
revenue out to Phase 4 has put a roster of other people in the first thing it
ships.

## What we concluded

That is a retention statement rather than a feature list.

A single personal chart is a one-time visit. Once a reader has seen their own
chart, the app is a calculator they close. A stored roster of other people is a
reason to come back, and it is the only element on the roadmap that **grows on
its own as the reader's life does**: a new partner, a new colleague, a child, a
parent who died and is still worth reading. Nobody has to be prompted to add
those. They arrive.

Co-Star's growth ran on the same primitive. Not the daily horoscope, which is
the visible product, but the friends layer underneath it.

## The ordering consequence

A people store is the cheapest item on any relational roadmap and the only one
with no pairwise cost. Everything directed sits on top of it: two records for a
composite, n records for a family, groups of records for a Union, and one record
for anybody's return chart other than the reader's own.

Shipping the store alone produces a usable and returnable app before a single
composite is computed.

**This does not add scope. It reorders it.** The reorder is the finding.

## What it does not license

Not the vocabulary. "Connection Chart" is their phrase for the two-person view,
"BodyGraph" is adjacent to a mark they hold, and a feature architecture being
unprotectable says nothing about what a thing may be called. See
`trademarks.md`, and the terminology register the founder has not yet initialed.
