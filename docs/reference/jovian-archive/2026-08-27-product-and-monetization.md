# humandesign.io: the product and how it is paid for

**Read:** 27 August 2026. **Source:** the founder's study of the same date.

## What they shipped

| Element | What it is |
|---|---|
| Core loop | Personal chart, then connection chart, then daily transit |
| AI layer | An assistant stated to be trained on 2,500+ hours of Ra Uru Hu's recorded teaching |
| Price | Stated as 100% free, always |
| Traction claim | 50M+ charts generated, 25+ years of data |
| Roadmap | Charts and AI, then education, then live sessions, then a certified-analyst marketplace |

## What we concluded

They are not monetising the product. They are using a free product to build the
demand side of a two-sided marketplace they intend to monetise several phases
out. The chart is the acquisition cost, not the revenue.

That is a coherent strategy for an organisation that owns a certification
pipeline and a roster of certified analysts. It is not available to a company
that owns neither, and copying the pricing without owning the back end is just
giving the product away.

## The part that cannot be copied, and why that is fine

The AI layer is possible because the corpus is theirs. There is no lawful route
to an equivalent corpus for inCommon: scraping lecture transcripts is both an
infringement and a quality trap, and an approximation trained on secondary
material would be a weaker version of a thing whose only claim is fidelity.

The useful reading is that this is a differentiation inCommon should not want.
Their axis is canonical authority. inCommon's is synthesis across systems and
psychology, framed as hypothesis rather than as doctrine, which is a different
promise to a different reader. Competing on their axis means losing on it.

## Where this landed in the build

- The truth-layer separation was already the safety rule. This study showed it
  is also the copyright boundary, so it is enforced once and serves twice.
- No compatibility score anywhere, which is category-standard and
  category-harmful.
- No streaks, which every competitor uses.
- Connection charts computed rather than quoted, with every word of
  interpretation written here.
