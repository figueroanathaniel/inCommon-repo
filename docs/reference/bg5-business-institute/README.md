# BG5 Business Institute

Jovian Archive's commercial and certification arm. Referenced in the Circles and
Unions specification of 27 August 2026.

## Why it has its own file rather than sitting under Jovian Archive

Because the risk is a different kind. Jovian Archive holds marks and a corpus,
which is a boundary about names and text. BG5 sells a **paid credential**: an
"OC16 Large Business Consultant Certification". A vocabulary with a certification
attached to it is commercial territory that somebody is actively earning from,
which makes it the highest-risk naming on the relational roadmap.

## The terms this concerns

The group-analysis vocabulary. Penta, for a group of roughly three to five, and
WA or OC16 for a group of around sixteen, are coined proper nouns belonging to
that curriculum rather than ordinary field description.

The Circles and Unions specification proposes replacing them with ordinary
English: Circle and Union. Those replacements are **not adopted**. The
specification is explicit that nothing in its terminology register is adopted
until the founder initials it, and as of this writing it is not initialed.

## The rule that follows

Where a term is ordinary description, using it is fine. Where a term is a coined
proper noun tied to a paid credential, it is not, and the substitution has to be
approved rather than assumed. When in doubt the test is simple: could a person
outside this field have said the word without having taken the course.
