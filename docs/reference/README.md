# Reference: the organisations in this category

What this folder is, and what it is not.

**It is** what inCommon has learned about the other organisations working in this
space: what they shipped, what they own, what they charge for, and what their
choices imply for a product that has to live beside them. One folder per
organisation, named for the organisation.

**It is not** a directive. Nothing in here tells anybody to build anything. The
build prompts and the specifications live in `docs/` proper and in the founder's
own register; this folder is the evidence those documents were reasoning from,
kept separately so that a finding can be re-read later without re-reading the
decision that came out of it.

That separation matters more than it looks. A study and a plan age at different
rates: a competitor's roadmap is true on the day it is read and may be false in
a quarter, while a decision made because of it stays in the build. Keeping them
apart means a stale finding can be corrected without unpicking the decision, and
a decision can be revisited without anybody having to remember where the fact
came from.

## House rules for anything added here

1. **Say where it came from and when it was read.** A claim about another
   company with no date is a claim nobody can check later.
2. **Separate what they said from what we concluded.** Their published roadmap
   is a fact about their marketing. What it means for us is an inference, and
   inferences belong in a paragraph that says so.
3. **Record what is owned.** Marks, serial numbers, and corpora, with the
   registry citation where there is one.
4. **No copied text.** Findings are written in inCommon's own words. This
   folder must never become a place where somebody else's copy is stored and
   then accidentally reused.
5. **No em dashes**, the same as everywhere else in this project.
