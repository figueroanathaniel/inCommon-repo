Your five colours, four ways to spend them
2a and 2b share the same five hexes and differ only in which one carries the primary action: 2a leads with neon green and keeps purple for the second rank, 2b leads with purple and saves green for moments that should feel rare. 2c is 2a with every border switched from cosmic blue to silver, which adds a sixth colour to the set. 2d is 2a with purple pulled back to a single filled button carrying black type, and every other purple turned black. In 2a and 2b cosmic blue is structural, doing the atmosphere layer, the borders, and the resting glow, because at full saturation it is too dark to carry text. In 2c it keeps only the glow.

One question this palette forces
Green already means something in this app. Success states use #59B37D, and the epistemic tag for a calculated, checkable claim is green too. If neon green also becomes the primary action colour, a button and a verdict start speaking the same language. Two ways out: retire the muted success green and let neon green carry both meanings, since an action and a confirmation rarely sit adjacent, or keep success green and reserve neon for actions only, which is what 2b does structurally. Crisis red is unaffected either way, and now reads as clearly separate.
