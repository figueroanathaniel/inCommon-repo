A keyword router is a first gate, not a diagnostic tool — it will miss oblique phrasing and flag some safe messages. By design it fails toward the human: BLOCK and HUMAN_RESOURCE both surface crisis lines (988, Crisis Text Line, DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233, NEDA 1-800-931-2237, SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357, Poison Control 1-800-222-1222). The real Oki runs downstream of this gate and never sees blocked messages. Console: SafetyRouter.safetyRoute(msg, history).
