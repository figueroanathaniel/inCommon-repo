The wiring, as a field

Two skies are computed for you: the moment of your first breath, and the moment the Sun stood eighty eight degrees earlier, which the tradition reads as the unconscious design. Every gate comes from the shared ephemeris and the shared wheel, every channel and centre from the shared topology, and every sentence from the shared atlas. Type, authority, and strategy are derived from the defined centres by the standard rules, stated in the footer.

The time is read as your device local time and no birth place is asked, so angles and houses are not part of this page. With no time, the chart is cast for noon local and labelled as that assumption, the same noon rule the app uses for an unknown time.

Prototype Human Design Field.html. Sky: app/ephemeris-backend-current.js (self contained analytical ephemeris) through app/ephemeris-points.js. Gates and lines: app/hd-wheel.js (302 degree origin, half open boundary rule). Channels and centres: app/hd-topology.js. Design moment: app/arc-solver.js designMoment(), bisecting the Sun to 88 degrees earlier rather than using a flat day count, because the Earth is fastest in January and slowest in July and 88 degrees is 86.6 to 92.0 days. Text: app/hd-atlas.js. Activations: every basic, node, and derived point in the registry at both moments; a channel is defined when both of its gates are present, a centre when it holds a gate of a defined channel. Type follows the standard wiring rules: no defined centres reads as Reflector, a defined Sacral reads as Generator, or Manifesting Generator when another motor also reaches the Throat, no Sacral with a motor to the Throat reads as Manifestor, otherwise Projector. Authority follows the usual hierarchy over defined centres: Solar Plexus, then Sacral, then Spleen, then Heart, then G, then the Lunar rule for a Reflector, otherwise none inner. The vendored app/three.module.js (r184) renders the field; a 2D canvas carries the same drawing when WebGL is absent.
