The number field

Every number this page shows is computed on the device by the shared modules, and every sentence is read from the shared content layer. The field is a portrait of those numbers: the Life Path at the centre, the name numbers on their orbits, and the current year, month, and day held in the band around them.

Numerology reads the name and the calendar only. A birth place is not part of any number on this page.

Prototype Numerology.html. Engine: app/incommon-core.js (InCommonCore, digit reduction preserving 11/22/33, life path, personal year and month). Text: app/numerology-content.js (NumerologyContent, universal, position, stage, and list readings). Personal day, pinnacles, and challenges are the standard method composed from the same reduce(): personal day is personal month plus day of month, first pinnacle is reduced month plus reduced day of birth, and the first pinnacle closes at 36 minus the life path. Stage readings follow the shared stageKey: under 28, under 56, then mature.
