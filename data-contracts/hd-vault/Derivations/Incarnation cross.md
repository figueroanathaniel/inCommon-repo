# Incarnation cross

**The app does not compute an incarnation cross.** No code names a cross (no
quarter, no angle, no cross title) for a chart.

What exists:

- The Atlas's Cross chapter (`HDAtlas.CROSS`: what, how, angles, caution) is
  writing about crosses in general.
- With a chart of the reader's own, the Atlas lights the four Sun and Earth
  gates (`crossGates`: personality Sun, personality Earth, design Sun, design
  Earth, from `hdChart().acts`) on its wiring and lists them under "In your
  chart".

If a cross is ever computed it would read exactly those four
[[Activations]] plus the [[Profile]] (for the angle). The 192 crosses are
deliberately out of this round (docs/hd-upgrade/02, section 4).
