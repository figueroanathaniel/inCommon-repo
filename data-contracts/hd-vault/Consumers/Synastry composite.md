# Synastry composite

The Commons tab: two charts overlaid. Builds both gate sets with `hdGatesFor()`
(from the reader's profile and from the partner's card or form), hands them to
`HDComposite.composite()` with the two time states, and draws
[[Composite row]] rows. The partner's chart is computed from their card on every
view; nothing reads [[computed_state]].
