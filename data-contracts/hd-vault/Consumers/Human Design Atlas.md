# Human Design Atlas

The Study room `/library/human-design-atlas`. Its entries are keyed
`chapter:slug` (`gates:g20`, `channels:20-34`, `centers:solar-plexus`), which
[[Chart address]] maps to.

| Reads | For |
|---|---|
| `hdChart()` (only for a chart of the reader's own) | "In your chart", the lit wiring, the Cross gates |
| `hdProfile()` | the reader's profile entry |
| `hdSkyNow()` | the sky ring and "In the sky now" |
| `HD_TYPE_LIGHT`, `HD_AUTH_CENTRE` | what an open type or authority entry lights |
| `HDAtlas`, `iching.js` | every word, the hexagram epigraph |

The Self-projected entry lights nothing: [[D-05 Self-projected authority lights no centre in the Atlas]].
